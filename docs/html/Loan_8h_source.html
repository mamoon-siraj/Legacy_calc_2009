5Formulas from: http://oakroadsystems.com/math/loan.htm
6
7Loan balance after n payments have been made:
8 B_n = A*(1+i)^n - (P/i)*((1+i)^n - 1)
9
10
11Payment amount on a loan:
12 P = i*A / (1 - (1+i)^-N)
13
14
15Number of payments on a loan: (You can use any logarithm base, as long as both logs use the same base.)
16 Aunt Sally offers to lend you $3500 at 6% for that new home theater system you want.
17 If you pay her back $100 a month, how long will it take?
18 Solution: 6% per year is 0.5% per month, or 0.005. P = 100 and A = 3500. N = 38.57
19 N = -log(1-i*A/P) / log(1+i)
20
21
22Original loan amount:
23 A = (P/i)*(1 - (1+i)^-N)
24
25
26Interest Rate:
27 i = (((1 + P/A)^(1/q) - 1 )^q - 1) NOTICE: This is an approximate not an exact solution
28 where q = log(1+1/N) / log(2)
29
30
31Variables:
32A the loan amount (the principal sum) or initial investment
33B_n or Bn (pronounced B sub n) the balance after n payments have been made. After the last payment has been made, B_N is zero.)
34i the interest rate per period, not per year
35 (For instance, if the loan payments are made monthly and the interest rate is 9%, then i = 9%/12 = 0.75% = 0.0075.)
36n the number of time periods elapsed at any given point
37N the total number of payments for the entire loan or investment
38P the amount of each equal payment
39*/
